# PM DNA delivery acceptance standard

The user's second illustrated reference is the quality target. The public sample must be an actual downloadable result from the deployed app, not an independently generated illustration with richer copy than the product can produce. Consistency means the same quality, hierarchy, font and visual system; visitors receive different evidence-based content, not identical claims.

## Required result

Exactly one map and one PNG. Retain the approved neutral avatars, varied pastel themes, PM-only taxonomy, white website and English output from multilingual input. No manual editor or second refinement form. Changes go through the original input and regenerate.

Each journey node has a PM role title and one or two visible evidence-backed challenge highlights. With rich evidence, choose two complementary highlights that communicate the hard or distinctive part of that role. Group title/highlights closely over the island; no detached paragraph floating far above it. Preserve dates only when known.

Each capability card has three levels: a short Title Case capability name; a professional judgement/decision sentence; an inset bubble with two or three complementary concrete practices when supported. The sentence captures an underlying capability or trade-off, not just a task. The bubble names actual methods, artifacts or mechanisms rather than generic work categories. Sparse evidence remains sparse or triggers a question; filling slots is never a requirement.

Use a rounded, substantial display face and rounded readable body type. The reference's exact font cannot be identified reliably from a raster image; use licensed Fredoka and Nunito to match its rounded character. Bundle fonts locally. PNG typography must match the preview; export uses the same font's glyph outlines so SVG-image font fallback cannot silently change it.

## Editorial rubric

Review six questions, based on the submitted experience:

1. Challenge: does the map reveal ambiguity, constraints, trade-offs or non-obvious product decisions that the user actually encountered?
2. Differentiation: could these words describe any PM, or do they identify this person's demonstrated professional pattern?
3. Abstraction: does the card express judgement and capability beyond paraphrasing CV tasks?
4. Specificity: do the practices name real evaluation artifacts, diagnostic lenses, product contracts, safeguards or other concrete mechanisms?
5. Provenance: does each role highlight/practice carry its own associated literal source quote, and do the capability's claims stay within demonstrated scope?
6. Presentation: is the hierarchy dense yet readable, rounded and free of foreground collisions in both phone and desktop layouts and the single PNG?

A polished phrasing alone does not establish truth. Citations validate provenance and selected specialist terms; semantic interpretation remains model-dependent. A rich CV should normally yield two role highlights and two to three practices per supported capability. A job title alone never proves that richness.

## Rejection examples

- `Select evaluation approaches and production prompts`: merely lists actions. Where model-limit/failure evidence exists, use a judgement lens such as `Judge model boundaries and de-risk real-world product fit`.
- `Turn repeated needs into reusable modules`: misses evidenced system constraints. When source supports it, foreground platform reuse versus reliability and extensibility.
- `Productisation` or `Operational optimisation` as the only bubble content: too broad. Identify the actual supported practice; do not invent one.
- Huge title/paragraph/island gaps, light condensed Arial, overflowing text, two result surfaces or a promotional sample produced outside the deployed renderer: fail acceptance.

## Release evidence

Verify both rich and sparse sources. Check role highlights, practice evidence, multilingual labels, capability differentiation, fonts in preview/export, actual painted bounds, one-map/one-PNG behavior, original-input regeneration, mobile layout and anonymous counts. Record automated checks and real-provider observations separately. Review the generated PNG itself before claiming sample fidelity. Existing quotas and storage policy remain unchanged.

Privacy & data

Your CV file is read in your browser and isn't saved by this app. To create or refine your DNA, extracted text and your instructions are sent to our service and OpenAI. Temporary results may be held in memory for up to 10 minutes for retries; refreshing clears your browser session.

We don't store CV files or maps in our app database. We keep aggregate creation/download counts and short-lived anonymous technical records for deduplication and abuse prevention. OpenAI's processing and retention policies also apply.

Technical details

Anonymous counter receipts expire after 48 hours and are cleaned up on the next counter access. Rate limits use a short-lived hashed network address in memory. A download count records an image handed to your browser; the app cannot verify that you saved it to disk.

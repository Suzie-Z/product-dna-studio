Privacy & data

CV text and maps are processed temporarily and are not saved by this app. Session history clears on refresh. OpenAI and your browser’s speech provider apply their own processing and retention policies.

We keep aggregate creation counts and anonymous request receipts to prevent duplicate counts. Receipts expire after 48 hours and are removed on the next counter access. We do not store CVs or maps in the counter database.

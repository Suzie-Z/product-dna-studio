Privacy & details

Your CV text and voice transcripts can contain personal information. Review them before sending. Files are parsed in your browser using libraries from a public CDN; scanned PDFs need OCR and are not supported. Voice transcription uses your browser’s speech provider and language settings; typing is always available. Generation, safety review and consented refinements send text to our Render backend and OpenAI. Common contact details are redacted before model processing, but automatic redaction is not exhaustive. The app does not intentionally persist or log experience text or results; they remain in browser/request memory. OpenAI and hosting providers apply their own processing and retention policies. Refreshing clears your draft and history. Download before leaving. No submission is publicly published. Usage protection stores short-lived counters, not CV content.

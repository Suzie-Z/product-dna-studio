Privacy & data

CV files are read in your browser. Extracted text and your instructions are sent to our API and OpenAI to create and refine your DNA. Server results remain in memory for up to 10 minutes for retries. Refresh clears your browser session. We do not write CVs or maps to the app database. OpenAI and optional browser speech services apply their own processing and retention policies.

We keep aggregate creation counts and anonymous request receipts to prevent duplicate counts. Receipts expire after 48 hours and are removed on the next counter access. We do not store CVs or maps in the counter database. Short-lived request limits use a hashed network address in memory.
